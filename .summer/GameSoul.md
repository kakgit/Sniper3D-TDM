Vision

You're building a tactical, grounded multiplayer shooter inspired by the precision and squad coordination of Delta Force Black Hawk Down. This is a focused, competitive experience where skill with a sniper rifle matters, tension stays high, and every match tells a story of intense team-vs-team combat.

Genre and Inspirations

Your game sits squarely in the action shooter space, drawing directly from Delta Force Black Hawk Down's DNA. What grabbed you most wasn't just the gunplay, but the tactical squad structure, the mission-driven approach, and the raw, responsive shooting mechanics. You're keeping that grounded, military authenticity alive in your own vision.

Look and Feel

This is a minimalist, tactical aesthetic. Think clean interfaces, sharp visuals stripped of unnecessary detail. The world is urban and gritty, grounded in realism rather than fantasy. No flashy effects or cartoon energy. Authentic weapon sounds puncture the silence. No pulsing soundtrack. Just the ambient tension of combat and the crack of accurate gunfire. The mood is serious, focused, and unadorned.

Core Mechanics

Team Deathmatch forms the backbone. Two teams spawn, engage, and when a player falls, they wait 20 seconds before respawning. A full session runs for one hour, allowing time for multiple rounds and momentum shifts. The sniper rifle is the centerpiece of your gunplay fantasy. Players spawn, hunt positions, coordinate with their team, and the cycle continues. It's pure competitive multiplayer: tight rounds, respawn windows that build tension, and one core weapon fantasy that defines every encounter.

What Makes It Special

Your hook is sniper-focused multiplayer with structured respawn cycles. By keeping respawn timers at 20 seconds and locking the format to exactly two teams in an hour-long session, you create a rhythm that's different from typical respawn shooters. The emphasis on the sniper rifle as the primary skill expression, combined with team-only play, gives this a distinct tactical flavor. This isn't a spray-and-pray arena. This is precision, patience, and teamwork under pressure.y
